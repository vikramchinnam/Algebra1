Discovery · Step 1 of 5
🍪 Cookie Time!
You have 12 cookies. Move the slider to share them equally among different numbers of friends. Watch what happens!
Friends: 3 Try 1, 2, 3, 4, 6
12 ÷ 3 = 4
💡 Notice something?
Total ÷ number of friends = cookies per friend. You just discovered division!
Equal Sharing · Step 2 of 5
🍎 Share the Apples!
There are 15 apples to share equally among 5 friends. Tap each apple to place it on the next plate!
15 ÷ 5 = 3 🎉
Equal Grouping · Step 3 of 5
⭐ Group the Stars!
Tap the stars to group them by 4. When a group of 4 is complete, a new one starts. How many full groups fit in 20?
20 ÷ 4 = 5 groups 🌟
💡 Different question!
Instead of "how many in each group?" we're asking "how many groups?"
